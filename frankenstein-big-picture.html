A. Put the events in order

Arrange the events in the order in which they happen in the story world (not the order in which the novel tells them). Use the arrows, then check. You may check as many times as you need.

    B. Mirror images

    Shelley builds Victor and the Creature as doubles. For each statement, decide: is it true of Victor, of the Creature, or of Both? One try each.

    C. Theme and idea: final reflection

    Choose one question and write a short argued answer.
